## Design Context

### Users
People seeking a screen-free tactile cue during meditation, breathing, transitions between tasks, or moments when attention scatters.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, tactile, and editorial. The experience should feel like moonlit polished metal: calm and deliberate, never clinical or overstimulating.

### Aesthetic Direction
Deep indigo-black space, moonlit silver, generous negative space, softened literary typography, and slow motion that follows user scroll. Avoid medical claims, neon, particle noise, abrupt camera moves, and autonomous animation.

### Design Principles
- Make one controlled movement the visual focus.
- Keep all language and controls live, readable HTML.
- Let user scroll set the pace; motion only interpolates their intent.
- Preserve a still, lightweight alternative for reduced motion and mobile.
- Treat the ring geometry as deterministic: one inner anchor, one moving band, nine fixed beads.
